GitHub announced the rollout of xAI's Grok 4.7 in Copilot, selectable across VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, JetBrains, Xcode, Eclipse, and the Copilot cloud agent. Enterprise admins can control access via model policy, and billing follows provider list pricing under usage-based billing.

For platform teams, that implies future internal evals, routing strategy, and compliance review should be designed around a swappable model layer.
