GitHub added an in-product validator for Copilot enterprise managed settings. It detects malformed JSON, unsupported configurations, invalid team mappings, and other issues that can prevent policy enforcement, with UI feedback showing affected files and JSON paths.

Why it matters: For engineering orgs rolling out AI coding at scale, the hard part is often not adoption but ensuring policies are actually enforced. This update signals a shift from documentation-driven config to built-in validation and observability, reducing silent policy failures and rollout drift.
